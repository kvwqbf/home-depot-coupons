## Which Home Depot coupons and promo codes actually work

The offers you can count on are the ones Home Depot runs itself: a $5 welcome discount for joining its email or mobile alert program, a year-round 10% military discount for verified service members and veterans, and category-level deals that change daily. Third-party "coupon codes" for extra percentages off sitewide are rare — most of what aggregator sites list are actually links to sales events, not codes you type in.

When you do have a real code, using it takes about ten seconds. At checkout on HomeDepot.com, go to the payment page, find the field labeled **Enter Promo Code**, paste the code, and click Apply. If it's valid, the discount comes off the order total immediately. If nothing happens, the usual suspects are an expired code, a category exclusion, a minimum spend you haven't hit, or a single-use code someone already burned through. Home Depot only allows **one promo code per order**, so there's no point hoarding three codes for a single checkout.

Two standing offers deserve specifics:

- **$5 off for new subscribers.** Sign up for Home Depot's email list or mobile alerts and you'll occasionally receive a $5 discount toward a first purchase. It's small, but it stacks with sale prices.
- **10% military discount.** Active-duty members, veterans, retirees, reservists, and their spouses (including Gold Star spouses) qualify for 10% off eligible purchases, year-round. Verification runs through SheerID online and takes roughly 10 minutes; once approved, the discount attaches to your account and applies automatically at checkout. In store, you pull up a QR code in the Home Depot app and scan it at the register. The cap is **$400 per calendar year**, which matters if you're planning a big renovation.

There's a conflict between these two offers that almost nobody mentions — it's in the next section, because it directly affects your cash back.

## Home Depot's own savings channels (where the big percentages live)

Most of the eye-catching discounts at Home Depot never involve a code. You shop the event directly:

- **Deal of the Day / Special Buy of the Day.** One rotating offer, 20–50% off, live for 24 hours. If you see a tool combo kit or appliance you were already planning to buy, this is often the deepest single-item discount available.
- **Savings Center.** Permanent clearance-style hub: bathroom fixtures up to 40% off, furniture and rugs up to 30%, lighting and ceiling fans up to 25%, vinyl flooring and water filters up to 20%.
- **Special Buys.** "Buy More, Save More" bundles and buy-one-get-one deals, mostly on tools and paint supplies. Multi-buy paint offers show up regularly — recent examples included 15% off when you buy four or more Wooster brushes.
- **Clearance and Overstock.** Discontinued and overstocked items routinely hit 60%+ off, with inventory turning over constantly.
- **Free shipping.** Over two million items ship free, with a $45 minimum for standard orders and $396 for large appliances.
- **Pro Xtra.** Free loyalty program for contractors and frequent buyers: member-exclusive pricing, a paint rewards program, bulk pricing, and early sale access. The Pro Xtra Credit Card adds up to $100 off a qualifying first purchase of $1,000 or more, plus a 2% discount on invoices paid within 20 days.

The seasonal calendar is predictable, which makes it easy to time a big purchase. Spring Black Friday lands in April with up to 70% off mulch, fertilizer, and gardening gear. Memorial Day covers grills, patio furniture, and appliances. September brings Decor Days, the year's largest home décor event. Black Friday and Cyber Monday take up to 60% off tools, appliances, and holiday goods, and the Christmas clearance starting December 26 reaches 75% off. If your purchase can wait for one of these windows, waiting usually beats any coupon code.
